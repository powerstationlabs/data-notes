# Changelog

## 2026.11.4 — 2026-11-01

### Added

- Initial release of the legacy-model reference set: 6 BLUETTI models (`ac200l`, `ac200max`, `bluetti-ac2a`, `bluetti-pioneer-md-ac180t`, `eb3a`, `eb70`).
- `lifecycle_status: discontinued` on every row.
- Reference runtime model (`tools/runtime.mjs`) and tests.
- Explicit attribution terms and third-party/trademark notice.

### Scope

- Legacy models only. Active models, current commercial offers, editorial content, translations, and the full production engine are documented on powerstationlabs.com.
- `warranty`, `noise_level`, `ups_switch_time`, and `release_date` are not included: warranty is region-specific and the other values need conditions that the source does not state cleanly.
- No brand logos, product images, manuals, prices, affiliate links, or internal identifiers.
